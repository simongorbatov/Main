Deal Lens

Open a listing, then click the Deal Lens icon in your toolbar.

Before your first check

When you ask for a check, Deal Lens sends the listing you're viewing to its server: the page text, links to up to four photos, and a screenshot of the page. An AI reads them and returns a verdict.

Nothing is sent until you click. Deal Lens doesn't store, sell, or advertise with what it reads.

Access code

Deal Lens is in private testing. Paste the access code you were given.

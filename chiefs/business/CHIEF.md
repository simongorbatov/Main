# Business chief — charter

Standing context for the business chief. The CEO maintains this file; the chief reads it first.

## Standing rules

- Only sell what ships in 3 days.
- Unshipped paid orders are always the first thing reported. Zero is the only acceptable number.
- Daily routine is 40 minutes: ship + answer (20), one TikTok (15), tomorrow's task (5).
- Max one one-time `[Store]` task per weekday. Weekends under 3 hours total.
- Notion is parked until Nov 1. Read it only when Simon asks about something specific.
